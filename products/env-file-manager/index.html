    Frequently Asked Questions

    How is my data encrypted? +
    We use the browser's built-in Web Crypto API to encrypt your environment variables with AES-256-GCM. Encryption happens client-side, so only your team has access to your data.
    Can I export my .env files? +
    Yes! You can export individual environment files or all projects at once. Export files are unencrypted .env files ready to use in your deployment pipeline.
    Is my data synced to the cloud? +
    Free plan: Data is stored locally in your browser (localStorage). Pro plan: Cloud sync coming soon with end-to-end encryption for team sharing across devices.
    How many team members can use Pro? +
    Pro plan supports unlimited team members. Generate shareable export codes to securely distribute environment variables to your team without storing credentials in version control.
    What happens when I upgrade to Pro? +
    Your free projects migrate automatically. You'll unlock unlimited projects, environments, and team collaboration features. Cancel anytime—no commitments.
    Can I use this in production? +
    Absolutely. The app is designed for production use. Store your production .env variables securely, manage access via export codes, and integrate with your CI/CD pipeline.
