Frequently Asked Questions

What is ASCII art?
ASCII art is artwork created using characters from the ASCII standard (letters, numbers, symbols). It's commonly used in code comments, README files, terminal greetings, and CLI tools.
What fonts are available in the free version?
The free version includes Standard, Banner, Digital, Block, and Bubble fonts. Pro gives you access to 30+ additional fonts including Shadow, Script, Slant, Speed, Avatar, and more.
Is this a subscription?
No! Pro is a one-time payment of $19 for lifetime access. No recurring charges, no hidden fees.

Yes! Once generated, the ASCII art is yours to use in personal or commercial projects, including client work, open-source projects, and products.
