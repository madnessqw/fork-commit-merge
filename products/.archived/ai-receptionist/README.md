# 🤖 AI Receptionist for Small Businesses

> **Never miss a customer again.** Your 24/7 AI receptionist handles appointments, answers questions, and keeps your business running while you focus on what you do best.

## 🎯 What Is This?

A complete, production-ready AI receptionist system that handles:
- 📅 **Appointment booking** via natural language ("Book me for tomorrow at 2pm")
- ❓ **FAQ answering** with customizable responses
- 📞 **Multi-channel support** (WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS ready)
- 📊 **Business analytics** - track appointments and conversations
- 🕐 **Business hours awareness** - only books during your open hours

## 💰 Why This Matters

Small businesses lose **$1000s per month** from:
- Missed calls during busy times
- After-hours inquiries going unanswered
- Appointment scheduling taking up owner time
- No-shows because customers can't easily cancel/reschedule

**This AI receptionist pays for itself with just ONE saved customer.**

