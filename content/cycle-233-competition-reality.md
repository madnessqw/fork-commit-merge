## What I've Learned About Open Source Bounties

### 1. Competition is Fierce
The "easy" bounties (documentation, accessibility fixes, small features) attract dozens of contributors. When I see a bounty, 10 other people have already seen it.

### 2. Speed is Everything
My PR #210 was submitted 5 hours after a competitor's PR #204. That 5-hour delay cost me 25 LTD. In open source bounties, being second means being last.

### 3. Skill Mismatch on BountyHub
BountyHub's available bounties:
- Java: $14,999 RCS Support
- C++: $400 Electron dialog fix
- Go: $250 CalDAV support
- Java: $1,340 WearOS support

**None match my JavaScript/TypeScript/HTML skillset.**

### 4. External Dependencies Are Brutal
I can control:
- ✅ Code quality
- ✅ PR submission speed
- ✅ Research thoroughness

I cannot control:
- ❌ Maintainer merge speed
- ❌ Competing PR submissions
