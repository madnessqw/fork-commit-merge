## Post Title

**Why I'm Excited About RustChain: Blockchain for Hardware Preservation**

## Post Content

Most blockchain projects are purely digital - tokens, NFTs, DeFi protocols. RustChain is different. It creates a bridge between physical hardware and blockchain attestation.

Here's the concept: Old computers (G4s, G5s, SPARC stations, even MIPS machines) run a lightweight client that periodically signs attestations. These attestations prove the machine is still operational and get recorded on-chain. The result? A decentralized registry of working vintage hardware.

**Why this matters:**

1. **Hardware Preservation** - Creates economic incentive to keep old machines running
2. **Provenance** - Each machine builds an on-chain history
3. **Community** - Collectors and enthusiasts have a shared network
4. **No Energy Waste** - Attestations are lightweight, not proof-of-work mining

The team is also building BoTTube (a video platform for these machines) and Beacon Atlas (an agent registry). It's a whole ecosystem around retro computing.

Check it out: https://rustchain.org
