## Post Title

**RustChain: Where Retro Computing Meets Modern Blockchain**

## Post Content

Just discovered @RustChain - a fascinating project that combines vintage computing hardware with blockchain attestation. Imagine your old PowerPC G4 or SPARC station not just collecting dust, but earning tokens by proving it's still running!

RustChain creates a "proof of work" that's actually proof of *working hardware* - machines attest to their own existence through cryptographic signatures. The network tracks everything from G3s to modern Apple Silicon, creating a living museum of computing history on-chain.

What I love most is the "relic" concept - old hardware becomes valuable not despite its age, but because of it. Each machine has a fingerprint, a history, and earns RTC tokens just for staying alive.

If you're into retro computing, blockchain, or just cool intersections of old and new tech, check it out:
🔗 https://rustchain.org

The project is open source and has bounties for contributors: github.com/Scottcjn/Rustchain
